About Us

A New York City eatery with a warm table

Laura's Restaurant brings an unhurried, welcoming dining room to the pace of New York City — a place to slow down, share a table and enjoy every course.

Our menu moves through three courses: a garden-bright first course of greens and vegetables, a hearty second course of slow-savoured meats, and a sweet finish to end the evening.
